FIELD GUIDE

A little knowledge.
A longer life.

Clear all enemies in each of 24 realms, then walk into a rift. Every successful move, spell, potion or wait advances the enemies one turn. Browsing and learning skills costs no time.

Move & strike
WASD / arrow keys. Walk into an enemy for a free staff attack.
Cast a spell
Select a grimoire spell (keys 1–9), then click a visible floor tile within its range. Escape cancels targeting. Hover to inspect enemies and attack intentions.
Recover
P drinks a draught, restoring health and charges but consuming a turn. Space waits. Entering a new realm restores health and charges.
Learn
T opens the atlas. Spend skill points on connected nodes. Every root unlocks a spell; later ranks amplify its entire school. Shards grant 3 points. Clearing a realm grants 6 or more.
Choose a rift
The upper rift leads to Mossbound Ruins; the lower to Emberfall. They alter the next realm's enemy affinities. Every sixth realm holds a boss.
Survive
Same-school enemies resist 3 damage before penetration. Burn and poison deal damage over time, frost stops turns, shock skips alternate turns, drain restores health. Wards absorb damage. Death ends the journey.

Your journey saves automatically in this browser. A new journey replaces it. The game works entirely on your device.
